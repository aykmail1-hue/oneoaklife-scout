OneOAKLife Scout Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

OneOAKLife Scout ("Scout") is a seller-operated tool used to prepare and manage listings for the OneOAKLife eBay store. This policy describes how the tool handles information.

Information used

Scout uses seller-supplied inventory details, product photos, descriptions, costs, asking prices, and other listing settings. When the seller authorizes access through eBay, Scout may use OAuth credentials to perform approved listing-management actions on that seller's behalf. We may keep seller-maintained sales totals or item sale prices for inventory accounting.

Buyer information

Scout's current listing-only workflow is not designed to retrieve or store buyers' names, mailing addresses, phone numbers, messages, or eBay account identifiers. Sale prices by themselves are not used to identify buyers. If the tool is expanded to handle orders or buyer information, this policy and applicable eBay data-handling requirements will be reviewed and updated before that functionality is enabled.

How information is used

Information is used to organize inventory, host listing photos, prepare listings, submit seller-approved changes to eBay, troubleshoot technical issues, and maintain basic operating records. Scout does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Service providers and security

The workflow uses Google Drive or Google Sheets for seller inventory files, Cloudinary for listing images, and Cloudflare for application hosting and security. Information placed in these services is subject to their respective processing and security practices. Sensitive application credentials are intended to be held in restricted application secrets rather than published in listings or on this page. No Internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and control

Seller-supplied inventory and operational records are retained as needed for listing management and accounting, and can be corrected or removed by the store operator, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements. OAuth access can be revoked through eBay. Technical request logs may be retained temporarily by infrastructure providers according to their configurations and policies.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or requests concerning information handled by Scout, email aykmail1@gmail.com.

OneOAKLife Scout is an independent seller tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by eBay.
